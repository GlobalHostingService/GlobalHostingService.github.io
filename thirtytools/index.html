Thirty Tools

30 handy tools for photos, videos, numbers and text

One small app instead of thirty

Thirty Tools puts the small jobs you do on your phone every week in one place: make a photo fit a form, shrink a video before you send it, work out a discount, count the words in a message, convert kilometres to miles.

Photos, videos and text are processed on your phone. They are not uploaded. You do not need an account.
